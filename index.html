Reducing friction without adding new features For this team project, we analyzed Go Mobile PGH and redesigned its structure to make everyday parking faster and less error-prone using AI. The challenge was to improve usability without inventing new functionality. Our prototype condensed the core parking flow from seven screens to one primary screen by intelligently pre-filling existing information, while still letting users review and edit every detail. We also considered feasibility, transparency, accountability, fairness, and privacy in the proposed AI-assisted experience.
